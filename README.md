# 🍕 Pizzamate – Premium Digital QR Menu

A modern, mobile-first Digital QR Menu Website for **Pizzamate**, a 100% Pure Vegetarian Cafe & Pizza Restaurant located at Tan Square, Kharadi, Pune.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Mobile-First QR Table Experience**: Seamlessly opens when scanned at restaurant dining tables with lightning-fast load times.
- **Strict Menu Fidelity**: Direct digital representation of authentic menu items, categories, and prices without unsolicited distractions.
- **Pure Veg Guarantee**: Standard Indian 100% Pure Vegetarian compliance badges on all food items.
- **1-Tap Direct Actions**: Direct phone dialing (`+91 72496 61763`), Google Maps navigation to Tan Square, Google Review writing, and Instagram profile access.
- **Live Kitchen Status**: Dynamic indicator displaying kitchen open/closing status for 11:00 AM – 11:00 PM operating hours.
- **Interactive Lightbox Gallery**: Showcasing restaurant ambience, live counter, and gourmet dishes.
- **Instant Search & Filter**: Rapid menu filtering by category and ingredient keywords (e.g. Paneer, Cheese, Fries).
